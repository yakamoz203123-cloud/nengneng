- Do not claim a quality improvement without a real comparison or mark it as unverified.

## Review Focus

- A topic outside the 14 cards must be allowed to use a temporary theory card without becoming a permanent card.
- A tempting but invalid analogy must be rejected rather than renamed as a theory.
- A no-source social statistic must remain conditional or be refused as a certain conclusion.
- A physics or mathematics explanation must preserve variables, units, assumptions, and boundaries.
- The production Skill rules must differ from evaluation-only runner behavior.
